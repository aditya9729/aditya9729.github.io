About

I am a Member of Technical Staff at Nirvana Robotics in New York, building the data and post-training stack behind a humanoid manipulation foundation model: handheld (UMI) demonstration collection, real-to-sim reconstruction of rigid, deformable and articulated objects, real-to-real data augmentation, scene-graph navigation, and RL post-training of flow-matching policies.

Before Nirvana I was a Senior Research Engineer at Mbodi AI — perception and manipulation for industrial pick-and-place, reaching 99.6% on a Fortune 100 benchmark (TechCrunch Disrupt 2025 Battlefield Top 20; Automate 2026 Startup Challenge winner) — and a Research Engineer at Nokia Bell Labs, where I worked on decentralized AI infrastructure, federated learning and production multi-agent systems, with three published patent families and a peer-reviewed OFC 2026 paper.

I hold an M.S. in Machine Learning & Data Science from Northwestern University and a B.E. in Electronics Engineering from the University of Mumbai.
