The Glasshouse↗

I worked on interpretable ML, studying how human intent shapes the reasoning of intelligent agents in cooperative and competitive settings. Circuit tracing was one of the approaches I explored. In this game, human intent and agent incentives are separate controls. Two energy cells can be split equally, relayed to the faster courier B, or kept by A. The numbers are authored utilities, not probabilities. The circuit is hand-wired: its features, weights and action scores are authored, not extracted from a trained model. Muting the intent features is a controlled intervention in that toy circuit, not real-model circuit tracing. The intact and muted panels hold the scene, incentives and weights fixed. A changed score need not change the selected action. These outcomes do not characterize all cooperative or competitive agents.

05

Nirvana Robotics · robot learning

Worlds Within

Ego retargeting · representation learning · contact · RL

Different arms. Same goal.↗

Ego retargeting: preserving task and contact information across viewpoint and embodiment changes. Two-link inverse kinematics drives the robot arm here; this is not a human-to-robot retargeting model.

Play the experiment →
Same thing. New world.↗

Data augmentation for representation learning. Match positive views of the same object despite changes in appearance, viewpoint and background. The embedding plot is an illustration of the desired invariance, not learned embeddings.

Play the experiment →
Soft worlds↗

Real2Sim2Real for deformable objects. A small Verlet cloth solver makes manipulation tangible. The real / twin / cousin labels are synthetic scene modes, not evidence of physical transfer.

Play the experiment →
Worlds with joints↗

Real2Sim2Real for articulated objects. Move a constrained hinge and change its environment. The angular constraint is simulated; no real hardware or reconstruction is involved.

Play the experiment →
A delicate balance↗

Contact dynamics for manipulated objects. The toy uses gravity, friction and a crush threshold. It omits most contact mechanics and is not a validated physics model.

Play the experiment →
A better next rollout↗

Post-training with RL and steering in dense trajectories. This mini-game uses a tiny bandit-style value update over path offsets with collision-based rewards. It is not deep RL or VLA/WAM post-training. Steering, feedback, update and fresh evaluation are separate steps.

Play the experiment →
The dream engine↗

An interactive nod to world models, foundation-model architectures and planning. A small analytic projectile model makes predictions under an assumed wind; observations reveal model mismatch. This is not a learned world model.
